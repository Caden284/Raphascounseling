About Ann-Marie

A passionate advocate for dignity and respect.

Ann-Marie Blaise built her career on a foundation of resilience and curiosity. Growing up in Trinidad and Tobago, her early experiences with bullying fueled a deep interest in human behavior, which led her to a B.Sc. in Behavioral Science and an M.Sc. in Counselling Psychology.

Her journey includes roles as a Guidance Officer, lecturer, and Care Coordinator, culminating in her current position as a school social worker. She continues to champion the belief that every person, regardless of their background, deserves a voice and to be treated with dignity.

Questions

Is online counseling right for me?

If you're wondering whether this could help, you're not alone. Here are the questions people ask most before their first session.

Start with a consultation
Does counseling work as well online?

For most everyday concerns, such as stress, anxiety, low mood, grief and relationship difficulties, research shows online counseling works as well as meeting in person. Many people also find it easier to open up from somewhere familiar.

What kinds of concerns can Ann-Marie help with?

Stress and burnout, anxiety, grief and loss, relationship and family challenges, life transitions, workplace difficulties, and the emotional weight of serious illness, for patients and the families caring for them.

What do I need for a session?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera, a steady internet connection, and a private, quiet space. Headphones help. The video link is emailed to you when your booking is confirmed, so there's nothing to install.

I live in a different country. Can I still book?

Yes. Ann-Marie sees clients across the Caribbean, the Netherlands and beyond. Sessions are scheduled on Trinidad and Tobago time (UTC−4), and the booking calendar shows open times in your own time zone.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. Sessions are one-to-one on a private video call, and your information is protected as described in our Privacy Policy. The few legal limits to confidentiality are explained to you before counseling begins.

When is online counseling not the right fit?

If you are in crisis or in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital. Online counseling is not an emergency service, but Ann-Marie is glad to support you once you are safe.
