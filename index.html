About

I am Ann-Marie Blaise.

Counselor, educator, mother, grandmother, and a lifelong believer that dignity is the foundation upon which respect is built.

My story

I, Ann-Marie Blaise, was born on July 26th, 1961 in the island of Trinidad and Tobago as the last of nine children to my mother. My early years, as I recall, were ones of just sufficient, never extras. My mother, a single parent, ensured that we were clean, fed and schooled. I grew to appreciate the sacrifices she made and embraced every opportunity given to ensure that I received maximum benefit of what she had to offer.

At Primary and, to a lesser extent, Secondary school, I was the recipient of bullying, primarily due to the color of my skin and the texture of my hair. This resulted in me having very few friends, which proved to be a blessing in disguise as I was plunged head on into my studies, often surfacing at the top of my class or close enough to the top. These negative experiences, however, fuelled thoughts about why people behave the way they do, why they treat others in such a demeaning manner.

While my mom and siblings encouraged me to get into a career of nursing, I preferred to delve a little deeper into the thought processes of humankind. This was influenced partly because of my being reared in a single-parent household and the challenges that came with that, coupled with the fact that I felt marginalized, being a person of color with short 4C hair, coming from a home of lower socioeconomic status and having to work doubly, triply hard to be seen and heard, not only by my peers but also by some teachers in both Primary and Secondary school. I spent 15 years in the education system as a Primary school teacher before pursuing my first degree, a B.Sc. in Behavioral Science, followed by an M.Sc. in Counselling Psychology. My training as a Primary school teacher whetted my appetite for greater knowledge.

While remaining in the field of education, I switched professions in 2007, becoming a Guidance Officer, a position I held for 12 years. I have also been an adjunct lecturer at one of the nation's Universities in the School of Social Sciences for 10 years. In 2019, I accepted a position as Care Coordinator, a position I held for 5 years on an island in the Dutch Caribbean. This heightened my awareness, knowledge and expertise in the field, as I was given the opportunity to travel to Holland to be exposed to different methods. I am currently the school Social Worker at another school in the same region.

I am the proud mother of three adult daughters and the grandmother to two grandchildren. I enjoy passing my free time by sewing, a skill I am certified in from one of our technical institutes; singing, which I partially inherited but went on to master through vocal training; doing Sudoku; and walking. I believe that all persons, regardless of their skin color or background, should have a voice and be treated with dignity and respect, as dignity is the foundation upon which respect is built.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

Welcome to the website of Rapha's Counseling. We are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal and protected health information, both through this website and in the course of providing our therapeutic services. By using this website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

A. Website visitors

  • Log data: your IP address, browser type, operating system, and the pages you visit. We use this to analyze traffic, improve functionality, and ensure security.
  • Cookies: small data files stored on your device to enhance your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.
  • Contact form submissions: your name, email address, phone number, and message, used solely to respond to your inquiry.

B. Clients and prospective clients

  • Contact information: name, address, phone number, and email.
  • Demographic information: date of birth, age, gender, and other relevant personal characteristics.
  • Health and medical information: presenting issues, health history, diagnoses, treatment plans, and session notes.
  • Billing and insurance information: insurance provider, policy number, and billing details.
  • Other information you choose to share during your therapeutic journey.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide therapeutic services: delivering high-quality, personalized care.
  • To communicate with you: scheduling appointments, sending reminders, and responding to inquiries.
  • For billing and administration: processing payments, filing insurance claims, and managing your client file.
  • To improve our website: analyzing trends and enhancing the user experience.
  • Legal and ethical obligations: complying with laws, ethical guidelines, and professional standards.

3. Confidentiality and disclosure of protected health information

Your information will not be disclosed to anyone without your written consent, with the following exceptions as permitted or required by law:

  • Duty to warn and protect: if we believe there is a clear and imminent danger to yourself or others, we are obligated to take steps to ensure safety, which may include disclosing information to relevant authorities.
  • Abuse and neglect: we are mandated reporters for suspected or actual abuse or neglect of a child, elderly person, or disabled person.
  • Court orders: a valid court order or subpoena may require disclosure of records.
  • Supervision and consultation: we may consult other professionals to improve care, with all identifying information removed.
  • Billing and insurance: limited information may be shared with your insurance provider to process claims.

4. Your rights regarding your information

  • Access your records: request a copy in writing and we will provide it in a timely manner.
  • Request an amendment if you believe your records contain incorrect information.
  • Request confidential communications in a specific way or at a specific location.
  • Request a restriction on how certain information is used or disclosed. We will carefully consider every request, though we are not required to agree to all of them.
  • File a complaint with our office or with the relevant government body if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

5. Data security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, including passwords, firewalls, and encryption for digital records, and locked, secure storage for physical records.

6. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2025

We at Rapha's Counseling are working to make our site, raphascounseling.com, accessible to people with disabilities. We believe that mental health support should be available to all, and we are dedicated to making our digital presence as inclusive as possible.

1. Our commitment to accessibility

Our goal is to meet and, where possible, exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. We are committed to:

  • Continuous improvement: we regularly review the website to identify and resolve barriers.
  • User-centered design: accessibility is built in from the beginning.
  • An inclusive experience for people who use screen readers, magnifiers, and voice recognition software.

2. Key features and measures

  • High contrast color palette and resizable text that works with browser zoom.
  • Clear fonts and a logical heading structure for screen readers.
  • Keyboard navigability for every link, button, and form.
  • Descriptive links and alternative text for all non-decorative images.
  • Captions or transcripts wherever video or audio is used.
  • Clearly labeled form fields with helpful error messages.

3. Known limitations

  • Third-party content: scheduling platforms and payment processors are chosen carefully, but we cannot guarantee the accessibility of their platforms.
  • Legacy content: some older content may not fully conform; we are actively reviewing and updating it.

4. Feedback and support

If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us by email at galemarieblades@gmail.com or by phone at +599 416 7333. We take all feedback seriously and will respond in a timely manner.

5. Accessibility for our therapeutic services

  • Telehealth options for clients with mobility challenges or who prefer to attend from a comfortable, accessible location.
  • Communication preferences: we are willing to discuss and adapt communication methods to suit your needs.

Thank you for visiting Rapha's Counseling. We are dedicated to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for all.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

These terms explain how sessions with Rapha's Counseling Center work, so you know what to expect before you begin. By booking a session or using this website, you agree to them. If anything is unclear, please ask before your first session.

1. Our services

Rapha's Counseling Center offers online counseling, consultation, professional development, group sessions, and palliative care support, provided by Ann-Marie Blaise, M.Sc. Counselling Psychology. Sessions are held by secure video call unless we agree otherwise.

Counseling is a collaborative process. Ann-Marie will share her professional judgment and recommendations, but outcomes cannot be guaranteed, and progress depends in part on your own participation.

2. Not an emergency service

Counseling is not a crisis or emergency service, and messages are not monitored around the clock. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, or you are thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital right away.

3. Booking and confirmation

  • New clients begin with a 30-minute Consultation Session, so we can understand your needs and agree on the right service.
  • A session is scheduled once you receive a confirmation by email or WhatsApp. Please check that the date, time, and time zone are correct.
  • Session lengths are fixed as listed on the Services page. Sessions end at the scheduled time, even if they start late.

4. Fees and payment

Current fees are shared with you before your first paid session. Payment is due before the session begins unless we agree otherwise in writing. Fees may change from time to time, and you will be told of any change before it applies to you.

5. Cancellations, rescheduling, and missed sessions

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule, by WhatsApp or email.
  • Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without notice, may be charged in full.
  • If you are more than 15 minutes late without contacting us, the session may be treated as missed.
  • Exceptions are made for genuine emergencies and illness at the practice's discretion.
  • If Ann-Marie needs to cancel, you will be offered a new time or a full refund.

Refunds are covered in the Refund Policy.

6. Online sessions

  • You are responsible for a stable internet connection, a working camera and microphone, and a private, quiet place for your session.
  • If the connection drops, Ann-Marie will try to reconnect or call you. Time lost to a problem on the practice's side will be made up.
  • Sessions may not be recorded by either party without the written agreement of both.

7. Confidentiality

What you share in counseling is kept confidential and handled as described in the Privacy Policy. The limits of confidentiality, such as a risk of serious harm to you or others, or the suspected abuse of a child or vulnerable person, are explained there and discussed with you at the start of counseling.

8. Minors

Clients under 18 need the consent of a parent or legal guardian before counseling begins. The guardian may be asked to attend part of the consultation.

9. Contact between sessions

WhatsApp and email are for scheduling and brief practical questions. Messages are answered as soon as reasonably possible, but are not a replacement for a session.

10. Ending counseling

You may end counseling at any time. Ann-Marie may also end or refer you to another professional if she believes a different service would better meet your needs, if fees remain unpaid, or if these terms are repeatedly not followed. Wherever possible this will be discussed with you first.

11. Website content

The information on this website is general and is not a substitute for professional advice about your own situation. The text, photographs, and logo belong to Rapha's Counseling Center and may not be copied without permission.

12. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We want the refund process to be as clear and fair as the rest of your experience with us. This policy works together with the cancellation terms in our Terms & Conditions.

1. Full refunds

  • You cancel a prepaid session at least 24 hours before it starts.
