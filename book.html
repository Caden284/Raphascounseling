Good to know

Are you a first-time client?

Yes, please book a Consultation Session first. It is a 30-minute conversation to understand your needs before we plan any ongoing therapy.

Where do sessions take place?

All services are available online by video, so you can join from wherever you feel safe and private. Ann-Marie works from the Dutch Caribbean and serves clients across the Caribbean, the Netherlands, and beyond.

What does “Request to book” mean?

You choose an open date and time and enter your name and email. You'll get a confirmation email with the video link for your session, and Ann-Marie will reach out personally if anything needs to change.

What if I'd rather talk first?

Message or call on WhatsApp at +599 416 7333, or email galemarieblades@gmail.com. Either is a perfectly good way to begin.

Prefer to reach out directly?

Every message is read and answered personally by Ann-Marie.
